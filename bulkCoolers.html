Overview

Fast, even cooling — by design

Direct expansion means the refrigerant evaporates inside the jacket itself rather than through an intermediate chilled-water loop. Paired with a laser-welded pillow plate, that gives a large, uniform heat-transfer surface and a shorter pull-down time on every collection.

  • Application: cooling milk to a range of 3 °C to 4 °C
  • MOC: SS304 / SS316 inner shell, to customer requirement
  • Laser-welded dimple jacketed sheet for faster cooling and efficient heat transfer
  • Designed to be user friendly for installation and operation
  • Conforms to ISO 5708 2AA II — latest version
  • Energy efficient and simple design, cleanable with CIP
  • Customisable control panel with PLC and HMI options

Engineering detail

What's inside the tank

Every element is specified for hygienic dairy duty and for the realities of a collection centre — unstable power, hard water and untrained operators.

Laser-welded dimple jacket

A robust, durable pillow-plate construction that allows faster cooling through direct expansion and efficient heat transfer across the full wetted surface.

Agitator

Stirring keeps every litre in the tank at the same temperature, so the entire content cools adequately and rapidly rather than stratifying around the jacket.

Control panel

Digital temperature controller with automatic compressor cut-off as standard. The panel can be built around an HMI-controlled PLC for a fully automatic system.

Condensing unit

Emerson or Danfoss make condensing units of reputed manufacture, sized against the pull-down time your collection schedule demands.

CIP cleanable

Cleanable in place, with spray-ball cleaning fitted as standard on closed tanks and available as an option on open tanks.

Energy efficient

A simple design with fewer moving parts and no intermediate chilled-water circuit — lower running cost and less to maintain in the field.
