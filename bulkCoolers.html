How it works

What is a DX bulk milk cooler?

A Direct Expansion (DX) milk cooler, also called a bulk milk cooler (BMC) or milk chilling tank, is a refrigerated milk storage tank that cools freshly collected raw milk and holds it at the required storage temperature. Refrigerant circulates through cooling surfaces integrated with the tank, and heat passes from the milk through the stainless-steel surface to the refrigeration system. Tavron uses laser-welded pillow plate for this cooling surface.

They are also used by dairy cooperative societies, village collection centres and bulk milk collection facilities.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What capacities are available for Tavron DX milk coolers?

Tavron DX milk coolers are available in 300 L, 500 L, 1,000 L, 2,000 L, 3,000 L, 5,000 L and 10,000 L.

What material is used for the inner shell of the tank?

The inner shell is manufactured using high-quality SS 304 or SS 316 stainless steel to ensure hygiene and durability.

Can a Direct Expansion milk cooler be used for milk storage?

Yes. DX milk coolers are designed for cooling milk and maintaining it under refrigerated storage conditions before processing or transportation.
