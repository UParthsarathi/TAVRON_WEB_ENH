HTST Pasteurizer

  1. Products
  2. HTST Pasteurizer
  • HTST Pasteurizer Stands High Temperature Short Time Pasteurize
  • It Is One Of The Common Methods Of Pasteurization.
  • Heat Exchanger Plates Made Of Stainless Steel Is Used Where Product Flows On One Side While The Heating Media Flows On The Opposite Side To Increase The Product Temperature Temperatures To Minimum 74° C For At Least 20 Seconds, Followed By Rapid Cooling.
  • High Temperature Short Time Pasteurization Is A Continuous Process That Efficiently And Effectively Destroys Microorganisms In Milk Products.
  • An HTST Pasteurization System Is A Modular Unit That Includes A Plate-And-Frame Heat Exchanger, Stainless Steel Balance Tank, and Pumps, Holding Tube, Valves Piping And Controls.
  • Wide Range Of Capacity From 50lph To 30,000lph
  • Continuous Process Allows For Packing To Begin When Pasteurization Begins
  • Highly Energy Efficient
  • Minimal Chance Of Damage To Milk Product
  • Fully Utilized Regeneration
  • Equipment Is Easy To Clean And Sterilize
  • HTST Pasteurizer Is Used For Milk, Juice And Other Liquids.
  • The Plate Heat Exchanger Setup Is Provided With Glue Free Gasket, Hygienic Plates Of Ss304/Ss316, CIP Cleanable.
  • The Gaskets Are Of NBM/EPDM
