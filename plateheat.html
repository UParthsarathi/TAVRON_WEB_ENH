Why heat through plates

A plate heat exchanger moves heat inline, as the product flows, through corrugated plates. It works with a low approach temperature and a small hold-up volume, and its heat-transfer coefficient is high. That makes it the usual choice across the food industry for heating and cooling milk, ice cream mix and similar products.

Two ways to build one

Gasketed, in a battery

For all kinds of fluids, in a frame of SS clad or carbon steel. Wide-gap plates handle products with suspended solids.

Semi-welded

For condensing and evaporating refrigerant. Plate pairs are laser-welded on the refrigerant side, with no channel gasket there, and the low hold-up volume means less refrigerant in the system.
