About Us

TAVRON ENGINEERS is a professionally managed company with ISO 9001-2015 Certification. We started our journey in the year 2004, with Chennai as our base and have successfully completed many Turn Key projects in food processing industries across India. SOLUTION DEVELOPMENT ENGINEERING

The key aspects of an integrated system are the solution design and the operating plan. Our refined practical design methodology ensures that projects have a compelling business case and deliver the results that are expected.

To develop a solution, we use an agnostic approach with an operations focus. It includes current state analysis, operations planning, and strategic/tactical planning. The solution for your operation will include process improvements, best practice production solution, performance optimizing software, and material handling technology.

We have supplied Special Purpose, Custom Built, State of the Art, Cost Effective Dairy and Food process equipment’s with high quality, automation and also caters to stringent quality levels, specified by the customers, with repeatability and reliability.
