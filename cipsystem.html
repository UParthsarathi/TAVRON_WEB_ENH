How it works

How a Tavron CIP system is built

CIP cleans production plant automatically, without dismantling it or changing it substantially from its production state. Each system is sized by its number of tanks and its number of loops, the cleaning processes that run at the same time.

The modules are pre-assembled and compact enough to transport easily. The tanks are assembled on site next to them and connected with prepared piping. An operator panel runs the system, with the PLC cabinet on the skid. The modular design makes the system easy to customise and expand.

Advantages

  • Optimised cleaning times and less downtime
  • Lower personnel cost than manual cleaning
  • Lower chemical cost through controlled dosing
  • Cleaning parameters (flow rate, concentration, temperature) met and monitored
  • Operator-friendly HMI
