How we work

Design. Build. Implement. Support.

Tavron is your single source partner to manage and control the entire integrated engineering and process flow — from defining business objectives to long-term support over the operational life of the system.

01

Design

Process definition, heat and mass balance, layout and equipment sizing matched to your throughput and product mix.

02

Build

Manufactured in our own 20,000 sq.ft facility in Chennai — SS304/SS316 fabrication, laser-welded pillow plate, panel building.

03

Implement

Erection, piping, PLC/HMI and SCADA integration, commissioning and validation on your shop floor.

04

Support

Spares, retrofits, process improvements and long-term service for the physical life of the plant.
